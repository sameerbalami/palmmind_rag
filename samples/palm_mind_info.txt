Palm Mind Technology is an AI company based in Kathmandu, Nepal.

The company builds conversational AI products, including chatbots and voice assistants for businesses.
Its flagship product is an AI customer support agent that answers questions in Nepali and English.

Palm Mind runs a paid AI internship program. The internship lasts three months.
Interns work on retrieval-augmented generation, LLM fine-tuning and backend APIs with FastAPI.
Interns receive a monthly stipend and may receive a full-time offer after the internship.

Office hours are Sunday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 5:30 PM. Interviews are held on weekdays between 10 AM and 4 PM.
